## 2. Becoming a Language Maintainer

We are actively seeking native speakers to contribute and maintain the following prioritized languages:
- **Spanish (`es`)**
- **Japanese (`ja`)**
- **German (`de`)**
- **French (`fr`)**
- **Portuguese (`pt` / `pt-BR`)**
- **Simplified Chinese (`zh-CN`)**
- **Traditional Chinese (`zh-TW`)**
- **Korean (`ko`)**
- **Indonesian (`id`)**
- **Hindi (`hi`)**

### Criteria for Language Maintainers:
1. Native or fluent speaker of the target language.
2. Willingness to review future translation updates or new strings added to the English catalog.
3. Adherence to our [Translation Guide (TRANSLATIONS.md)](TRANSLATIONS.md).

---

## 3. Adding Your Name to This Recognition List

When submitting a pull request that adds or substantially updates a translation catalog:
1. Ensure all CI validation checks pass (`node scripts/validate-locales.js`).
2. Add your name, GitHub handle, and target language to the table above in section 1.
3. Once reviewed and merged, your contribution will be permanently credited here, in our GitHub release notes, and in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

Thank you for your dedication to open-source software!
