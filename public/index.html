Zapwebp — Free SVG, PNG, JPG to WebP Converter

Zapwebp is a fast, 100% private in-browser image converter built for developers, designers, and web performance engineers. Convert transparent SVGs, high-resolution PNGs, photos, AVIFs, and WebPs in milliseconds with guaranteed alpha channel preservation, mathematical lossless verification, and zero server uploads.

Why Zapwebp?

  • 100% Local In-Memory Processing: Your visual data never leaves your device or touches a third-party server. Pure WebAssembly and Canvas execution.
  • Deterministic Alpha Preservation: Alpha channels are mathematically preserved. Transparent pixels never turn into black boxes.
  • Clean Metadata Stripping: Strip EXIF, GPS location, and camera hardware serials with a single click before sharing.
  • Instant Batch ZIP Bundles: Convert dozens of images at once and download everything in an uncompressed or high-speed ZIP archive.
  • Developer-Ready Ecosystem: Powered by the exact same open-source engine available as an MCP AI Agent server, CLI, and npm SDK.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Zapwebp completely free?

Yes. Zapwebp is 100% free and open-source under the MIT license. There are no subscriptions, no file size paywalls, and no account requirements.

Does Zapwebp upload my images to a server?

No. Zapwebp executes entirely inside your browser's local memory using Web Workers and WebAssembly. Your images never leave your machine.

Why does my converted image look sharper or smaller?

Modern WebP and AVIF formats utilize advanced intra-frame predictive encoding and Lanczos3 resampling, achieving up to 85% smaller file sizes than legacy JPEG or PNG while retaining visual fidelity.

How does Zapwebp protect image transparency?

Zapwebp checks the alpha channel of every source image. When converting to alpha-capable targets (WebP, AVIF, PNG), transparency is preserved byte-for-byte. If you target JPEG (which has no alpha support), Zapwebp prompts you for an explicit background color rather than corrupting transparent areas to black.

What formats can Zapwebp convert?

Zapwebp supports SVG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, JPEG, GIF, TIFF, and multi-size ICO containers with bi-directional conversion.
