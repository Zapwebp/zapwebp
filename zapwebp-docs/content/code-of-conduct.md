# Community Code of Conduct

Zapwebp is dedicated to providing an open, welcoming, and harassment-free environment for everyone participating in our project, repositories, discussions, and associated communication channels. We expect all contributors, maintainers, and community members to collaborate with empathy, professionalism, and mutual respect.

---

## 1. Our Community Standards

We encourage active participation and constructive engagement across all project channels. 

### Positive Behaviors
Examples of behavior that contribute to a healthy, productive community include:
- Demonstrating empathy, patience, and kindness toward fellow contributors.
- Welcoming newcomers and providing constructive, encouraging technical feedback.
- Respecting differing perspectives, experience levels, and architectural viewpoints.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism and focusing on what is best for the project.
- Showing accountability by acknowledging mistakes and working collaboratively toward solutions.

### Unacceptable Behaviors
Behaviors that disrupt or harm the community will not be tolerated, including:
- Offensive, derogatory, or discriminatory language, comments, or personal attacks.
- Harassment, stalking, or inappropriate personal attention in public or private communications.
- Publishing or threatening to publish others' private information (such as personal email addresses or physical locations) without explicit consent.
- Trolling, insulting remarks, or sustained disruption of technical discussions and pull request reviews.
- Any conduct that reasonably could be considered inappropriate or unprofessional in a software development environment.

---

## 2. Scope of Application

This Code of Conduct applies within all official Zapwebp community spaces, including:
- GitHub repositories, pull requests, issue trackers, and discussions under [`github.com/Zapwebp`](https://github.com/Zapwebp);
- Documentation feedback and translation channels;
- Public or private interactions where an individual represents the Zapwebp project or community.

---

## 3. Enforcement & Governance

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and pull requests that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

### Enforcement Guidelines
When investigating reported violations, maintainers will determine fair and proportional responses:

1. **Correction**: A private, written explanation providing clarity around the nature of the violation and guidance on appropriate conduct.
2. **Warning**: A formal warning detailing the consequences of continued unacceptable behavior, which may include temporary restriction from project interactions.
3. **Temporary Suspension**: A temporary ban from commenting, opening issues, or submitting pull requests across all project repositories for a designated period.
4. **Permanent Removal**: A permanent ban from all Zapwebp repositories, community forums, and communication channels for egregious, unrepentant, or severe violations.
